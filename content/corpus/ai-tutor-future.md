# Reed Hastings and Sal Khan on AI in education

In an April 2026 TED conversation, Why AI is an even bigger deal than you think, Khan Academy founder Sal Khan interviews Netflix co-founder Reed Hastings. Hastings says the theory behind tutoring is that every student would learn best with an individual human tutor, and that AI, which has improved quickly in a few years, may one day match and beat the human individual tutor (Hastings & Khan, 2026).

Hastings does not think teachers disappear. He describes three purposes of school: creating good citizens, giving children economic opportunity and socialization. Online tools are good mainly at the academic part, so he wants teachers to focus more on social and emotional learning, which he says moves teachers up the value chain (Hastings & Khan, 2026).

Asked about board meetings at an AI company, Hastings says the mission is not maximizing profit but getting humanity into the age of AI successfully, and that this will be very challenging (Hastings & Khan, 2026).

Our group member Abdulrahman Ahmad uses this talk as the clearest example of AI's potential in daily life: a personal tutor for each student, with people and AI working together.
