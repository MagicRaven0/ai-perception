# Group essay: AI's potential (Abdulrahman Ahmad)

Our group member Abdulrahman Ahmad wrote the research essay A.I.'s potential: Shaping up a united future. He argues that, with the right approach and careful expansion, AI can prove its value to the people who use it.

He draws on four sources. Witt (2026) shows how much influence AI leaders such as Nvidia's Jensen Huang have over how AI develops, and how Huang treats AI's challenges as engineering problems. Hastings and Khan (2026) describe a future where each student has a personal AI tutor. Mims (2026) argues that AI should take over some tasks while people keep the ones that need empathy, authenticity and transparency, and that AI should show its work so people can check it. McClain et al. (2025) show the large gap between optimistic AI experts and a more worried public.

He answers critics who say AI's risks outweigh its benefits by agreeing that AI can be risky if it is used carelessly, but arguing that it still has great potential when used with caution, limits and awareness. His essay fits our shared argument: people trust AI more when they understand it and feel in control of how it is used.
